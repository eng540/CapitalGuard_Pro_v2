## Remaining acceptance boundary

No real Telegram Export was imported, no Telegram user-account/MTProto/TDLib credential was configured, and no real historical market-data provider was connected in this slice. Therefore no historical analyst performance has been certified. The next acceptance step requires a user-provided authorized export or explicit connector decision plus a real point-in-time market dataset. Until then, historical records remain controlled, non-actionable, and excluded from public ranking by default.
